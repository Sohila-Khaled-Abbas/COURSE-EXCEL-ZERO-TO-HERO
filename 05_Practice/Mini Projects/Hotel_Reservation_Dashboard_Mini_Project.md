# Mini-Project: Hotel Reservation Analytics Dashboard

> [!abstract] Scenario
> Hospitality executives require visibility into reservation cancellation behaviors, lead time sensitivity, and revenue by room category across 36,000+ guest records.

## Project Deliverables
1. **KPI Cards**:
   - Total Bookings
   - Cancellation Rate % (`Cancelled / Total`)
   - Average Daily Rate (ADR)
   - Lead Time (Average days between booking and check-in)
2. **Interactive Visualizations**:
   - Donut Chart: Booking Status Breakdown (Confirmed, Canceled, No-Show).
   - Bar Chart: Revenue by Room Type (Deluxe, Suite, Standard).
   - Scatter / Trend: Cancellation likelihood vs Lead Time.
3. **Slicer Pane**:
   - Synchronized Slicers for `Booking Channel` and `Year`.
