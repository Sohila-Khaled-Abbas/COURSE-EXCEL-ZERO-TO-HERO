# Mini-Project: Workforce Attrition & Retention Modeling

> [!abstract] Scenario
> The Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO) requires an analysis of employee turnover across departments, salary bands, and tenure cohorts.

## Core Tasks
1. Extract and combine employee demographic data and historical exit logs using Power Query.
2. Calculate key HR metrics:
   - **Headcount**: Total active workforce.
   - **Turnover Rate %**: `Exits / Average Headcount`.
   - **Average Tenure**: Years of service before exit.
3. Construct a departmental scorecard in Pivot Tables with conditional formatting highlighting flight-risk business units.
