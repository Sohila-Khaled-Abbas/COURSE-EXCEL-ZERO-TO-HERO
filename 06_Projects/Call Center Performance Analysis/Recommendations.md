# 8. Strategic Business Recommendations for Claire

## 1. Implement Virtual Queuing & Automated Queue Callback
- **Operational Deficit**: 18.92% call abandonment rate resulting from queue wait times exceeding customer tolerance thresholds (ASA = 67.52s).
- **Recommended Action**: Implement an ACD (Automated Call Distribution) virtual callback feature offering callers whose expected wait exceeds 45 seconds the choice of an immediate callback when an agent frees up.
- **Projected Impact**: Reduces abandonment by 35–50%, prevents repeat inbound surges, and immediately lifts CSAT.

## 2. Workforce Management & Peak Shift Realignment
- **Operational Deficit**: Midday volume spikes (11:00 AM – 2:00 PM) create queue congestion where hold times climb above 80 seconds.
- **Recommended Action**: Reallocate the 8-agent roster with staggered lunch rotations and 100% station staffing during the 3-hour midday peak.
- **Projected Impact**: Lowers average speed of answer to under 45 seconds across all operational hours.

## 3. Targeted Peer Coaching & Quadrant Optimization
- **Operational Deficit**: Performance variance between highest-rated agent (Martha: 3.47 CSAT) and lowest-rated agent (Joe: 3.33 CSAT, 70.99s ASA).
- **Recommended Action**: Pair Joe with Martha in a structured mentorship rotation focusing on call de-escalation, rapid system navigation, and positive customer closing language.
- **Projected Impact**: Elevates team minimum CSAT to >3.40 within 45 days.

## 4. Self-Service Offloading for Streaming & Billing
- **Operational Deficit**: 40.5% of total call volume stems from routine payment inquiries (1,007 calls) and streaming account authentications (1,019 calls).
- **Recommended Action**: Deploy an Interactive Voice Response (IVR) self-service menu and mobile app push-authentication for simple billing confirmations and streaming password resets.
- **Projected Impact**: Deflects 15–25% of transactional inquiries from the telephone queue, freeing agent bandwidth for high-complexity technical inquiries.

## 5. Connecting Call Centre Experience to Proactive Customer Retention (Task 2 Synergy)
- **Strategic Insight**: In telecom, repeated calls with unresolved technical issues or long hold times are the #1 leading indicator of customer contract cancellation (Churn).
- **Recommended Action**: Feed call interaction data directly into the Customer Retention risk model (`02 Churn-Dataset.xlsx`) to flag accounts opening more than 2 technical tickets for proactive outreach before termination.
