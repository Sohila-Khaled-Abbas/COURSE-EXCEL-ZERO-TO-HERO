# 8. Strategic Business Recommendations

## 1. Implement Virtual Queue & Automated Callback
- **Problem**: 18.92% call abandonment rate due to long queue wait times (ASA = 67.5s).
- **Action**: Offer callers the option to receive a scheduled automated callback rather than holding in queue.
- **Expected Impact**: Reduces abandonment by 35-50% and improves CSAT by preserving customer time.

## 2. Workforce Management & Peak Staffing Alignment
- **Problem**: Midday call volume spikes cause wait times to climb over 75 seconds.
- **Action**: Adjust 8-agent shift schedules to implement staggered lunch hours and overlap staffing between 11:00 AM and 2:00 PM.
- **Expected Impact**: Decreases ASA to under 45 seconds.

## 3. Targeted Agent Coaching & Best-Practice Sharing
- **Problem**: Variance between highest-rated agent (Martha: 3.47) and lowest-rated agent (Joe: 3.33).
- **Action**: Pair Joe with Martha for de-escalation coaching and call-wrapping workflow optimization.

## 4. Enhanced Self-Service for Common Billing & Streaming Queries
- **Problem**: Over 40% of call volume is driven by recurring streaming and payment inquiries.
- **Action**: Introduce IVR self-service and mobile app FAQs for simple billing status and streaming re-authentication.
