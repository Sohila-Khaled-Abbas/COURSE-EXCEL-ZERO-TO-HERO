# 8. Strategic Business Recommendations Across All 3 Domains

> [!abstract] Actionable Transformation Roadmap
> Translating analytical findings into measurable enterprise value requires strategic alignment across operations, finance, and human resources. This roadmap outlines targeted initiatives, projected ROI, implementation timelines, and executive ownership for all three client divisions.

---

## 📞 1. Strategic Initiatives for Call Centre Operations (Claire)

### Initiative 1.1: Automated Virtual Queue & Immediate Callback
- **Problem**: 18.92% queue abandonment rate caused by hold times exceeding customer tolerance during midday peaks (ASA = 67.52s).
- **Action**: Implement an Automated Call Distribution (ACD) virtual callback feature offering callers whose expected queue wait exceeds 45 seconds the option of an automated callback when an agent frees up, preserving their position in queue.
- **Projected Impact**: Reduces abandonment by **35% to 50%**, eliminates repeated abandoned call redials, and immediately lifts CSAT by +0.30 points.
- **Timeline**: 60-day telephony IVR deployment.

### Initiative 1.2: Peak Workforce Shift Realignment
- **Problem**: Midday volume spikes (11:00 AM – 2:00 PM) experience 42% higher queue arrivals, driving wait times above 90 seconds.
- **Action**: Stagger the 8-agent lunch rotations and mandate 100% station staffing during the 3-hour midday peak.
- **Projected Impact**: Compresses average speed of answer to under 45 seconds across all operational hours without hiring additional headcount.
- **Timeline**: Immediate 14-day schedule adjustment.

### Initiative 1.3: Targeted Peer Coaching & Quadrant Optimization
- **Problem**: Performance variance between Martha (3.47 CSAT, thorough handle time) and Joe (3.33 CSAT, 70.99s ASA).
- **Action**: Pair Joe with Martha in a structured 30-day peer mentoring rotation focusing on call de-escalation, rapid CRM navigation, and positive closing phrasing.
- **Projected Impact**: Elevates team minimum CSAT to $\ge 3.40$ within 45 days.
- **Timeline**: 30-day coaching sprint.

### Initiative 1.4: Self-Service IVR Deflection for Streaming & Billing
- **Problem**: Routine payment questions (1,007 calls) and streaming credential resets (1,019 calls) account for 40.5% of total queue demand.
- **Action**: Deploy automated self-service SMS links and interactive IVR billing menus for simple balance inquiries and streaming password resets.
- **Projected Impact**: Deflects 15% to 25% of routine inbound traffic, freeing agent bandwidth for high-complexity technical inquiries.
- **Timeline**: 90-day digital portal rollout.

## 🔄 2. Strategic Initiatives for Customer Retention (David Chen)

### Initiative 2.1: Contract Migration & Loyalty Incentive Program
- **Problem**: Month-to-month subscribers exhibit a **42.71% churn rate**, accounting for 88.55% of all lost accounts ($123,000+ monthly revenue loss).
- **Action**: Launch an automated contract migration campaign offering month-to-month customers a $10/month bill credit or free streaming package in exchange for transitioning to a 1-year or 2-year commitment.
- **Projected Impact**: Converting 20% of month-to-month accounts to annual contracts saves **$24,800/month ($297,600 annualized)** in retained MRR.
- **Timeline**: 45-day commercial launch.

### Initiative 2.2: Fiber Optic Quality Assurance & Proactive Onboarding
- **Problem**: Fiber Optic subscribers suffer from a **41.89% churn rate** despite paying premium rates ($74.44 average monthly charge).
- **Action**: Implement a mandatory 30-day proactive technical health-check call for all newly installed Fiber Optic lines, paired with complimentary Wi-Fi optimization.
- **Projected Impact**: Reduces early Fiber churn by 25%, protecting high-ARPU subscriber accounts.
- **Timeline**: 60-day field operations rollout.

### Initiative 2.3: Automated Retention Trigger on Tech Ticket Escalations
- **Problem**: Customers opening $\ge 2$ technical support tickets churn at **63.4%** ($2.4\times$ the baseline rate).
- **Action**: Establish an automated event bridge between Call Center ticketing (Domain 1) and the CRM retention desk. When a subscriber logs their second technical ticket, route their call directly to senior tier-2 specialists and trigger a proactive retention credit outreach.
- **Projected Impact**: Reduces ticket-driven churn by 30%, retaining an estimated **$18,500/month** in at-risk revenue.
- **Timeline**: 30-day automated webhook integration.

### Initiative 2.4: Electronic Check to Auto-Pay Incentive
- **Problem**: Electronic check users churn at **45.29%**, compared to 15.24% for automated credit cards.
- **Action**: Offer a one-time $15 billing credit for customers migrating from electronic check to automatic recurring bank debit or credit card billing.
- **Projected Impact**: Mitigates billing friction and reduces customer churn across 2,365 accounts.
- **Timeline**: 30-day billing portal promotion.

## 👥 3. Strategic Initiatives for Human Capital & Diversity (HR Leadership)

### Initiative 3.1: Executive Sponsorship & Career Progression Tracks
- **Problem**: Female representation drops from 50.56% at Job Level 6 to **14.81% in executive leadership** (Tiers 1 & 2).
- **Action**: Establish a formal executive sponsorship program where C-suite executives and Directors mentor high-performing female Senior Managers (Job Level 3) for succession planning into Director and Executive roles.
- **Projected Impact**: Elevates female executive representation from 14.81% to **25.0% within 24 months**.
- **Timeline**: 6-month formal cohort rollout.

### Initiative 3.2: Promotion Calibration & Objective Performance Auditing
- **Problem**: FY21 female promotion rate (8.78%) lagged male promotion rate (11.19%), even among personnel with identical top-tier performance ratings.
- **Action**: Implement blinded promotion calibration committees and standardize evaluation scorecards to eliminate unconscious bias in career progression.
- **Projected Impact**: Closes the promotion velocity gap within the FY22 appraisal cycle.
- **Timeline**: Prior to FY22 annual performance cycle.

### Initiative 3.3: Junior Operational Grade Retention Program
- **Problem**: 85.1% of all employee turnover (40 of 47 leavers) occurs in Job Levels 5 and 6, with female turnover reaching 11.2%.
- **Action**: Introduce clear lateral career mobility paths, educational tuition reimbursement, and professional certification sponsorships for customer support and operational staff.
- **Projected Impact**: Compresses annual corporate turnover from 9.40% to **<7.0%**, reducing costly personnel replacement and retraining expenses.
- **Timeline**: 90-day HR policy rollout.
