# 7. Evidence-Based Analytical Findings for Claire

## Finding 1: Significant Inbound Abandonment (18.92%)
- Out of 5,000 incoming customer inquiries, **946 customers hung up** without speaking to an agent.
- The average speed of answer for answered calls is **67.52 seconds** (ASA). Customers who abandoned likely faced wait times exceeding 75–120 seconds during peak operational hours.
- Abandonment rate directly reduces customer retention and spikes downstream repeat calls.

## Finding 2: High Problem Resolution for Connected Calls (89.94%)
- Once a customer successfully reaches an agent, the First Contact Resolution rate is remarkably strong (**89.94%** of answered calls, 3,646 resolved).
- **Core Diagnosis**: Customer dissatisfaction is overwhelmingly driven by **queue wait bottlenecks and accessibility barriers**, not by agent technical incompetence or poor resolution knowledge.

## Finding 3: Balanced Workload but Noticeable Performance Disparities
- Call allocation across the 8 dedicated agents is evenly balanced (ranging from 582 to 666 calls per agent over Q1 2021).
- **Dan** achieved the highest answer rate (82.62%), while **Diane** recorded the lowest (79.15%).
- **Martha** secured the highest customer satisfaction rating (**3.47 / 5.00**), while **Joe** registered the lowest (**3.33 / 5.00**) paired with the slowest speed of answer (**70.99 seconds**).

## Finding 4: Inbound Topic Uniformity & Technical Complexity
Inbound demand is distributed almost uniformly across five primary functional topics:
1. `Streaming`: 1,019 calls (20.38%)
2. `Technical Support`: 1,004 calls (20.08%)
3. `Payment related`: 1,007 calls (20.14%)
4. `Admin Support`: 994 calls (19.88%)
5. `Contract related`: 976 calls (19.52%)

Technical support inquiries exhibit lower CSAT ratings and longer handle times due to hardware diagnostics and multi-step customer troubleshooting.

## Finding 5: The Agent Performance Quadrant (Handle Time vs Volume)
Evaluating agents across the 2-axis matrix (**Average Handle Time / Talk Duration vs Calls Answered**) reveals distinct behavioral profiles:
- **High-Volume Drivers (Jim & Dan)**: Jim answered 536 calls (team maximum) while maintaining a 90.49% resolution rate. Dan answered 523 calls with a 90.06% resolution rate and 3.45 CSAT.
- **Speed & Queue Clearing (Becky)**: Lowest ASA on the team (65.33s) and rapid handle time, providing crucial queue pressure relief during midday spikes.
- **Thorough Specialist (Martha)**: Longer talk durations correlate directly with the highest CSAT rating on the team (3.47), proving that customer satisfaction in technical areas rewards thoroughness.
- **Coaching Priority (Joe)**: Below-average call intake (484 answered), slowest response time (70.99s), and lowest CSAT (3.33), presenting an ideal candidate for peer mentoring.
