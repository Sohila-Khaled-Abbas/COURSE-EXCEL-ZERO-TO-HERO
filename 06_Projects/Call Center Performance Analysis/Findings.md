# 7. Evidence-Based Analytical Findings

## Finding 1: Significant Abandonment (18.92%)
- Out of 5,000 incoming calls, 946 customers hung up without speaking to an agent.
- The average speed of answer for answered calls is 67.52 seconds. Customers who abandoned likely faced wait times exceeding 60-90 seconds.

## Finding 2: High Resolution Efficiency for Connected Calls (89.94%)
- Once a customer reaches an agent, the resolution rate is remarkably high (nearly 90%).
- This indicates that customer frustration is driven by **queue wait times and accessibility**, not agent technical incompetence.

## Finding 3: Agent Workload & Consistency
- Call distribution across agents is evenly allocated (between 582 and 666 calls per agent over 90 days).
- **Dan** achieved the highest answer rate (82.62%), while **Diane** had the lowest (79.15%).
- **Martha** achieved the highest customer satisfaction score (3.47 / 5.00), while **Joe** had the lowest (3.33 / 5.00) alongside the slowest average answer speed (70.99s).

## Finding 4: Topic Distribution Analysis
Call volume is evenly distributed across five core categories:
1. `Streaming`: ~1,019 calls
2. `Technical Support`: ~1,004 calls
3. `Payment related`: ~1,007 calls
4. `Contract related`: ~976 calls
5. `Admin Support`: ~994 calls
Technical support inquiries exhibit slightly lower CSAT due to troubleshooting complexity.
