# 1. Business Context & Problem Statement

## The Business Context & PwC Digital Accelerator Engagement
Within the **PwC Switzerland Virtual Case Experience**, you serve as a **Digital Accelerator** embedded within a client-facing technology advisory team. Our client—a major telecommunications provider—operates an inbound customer contact center handling technical support, payment and billing inquiries, contract administration, general admin, and streaming services.

**Claire**, the Call Centre Manager, has reached out to PwC to help her department transition from intuition-based supervision to data-driven operational management. Claire requires an intuitive, self-service dashboard (built in Power BI / modern Excel) reflecting all relevant Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) and metrics in the dataset so her leadership team can better understand both customer behavior and agent productivity.

## The Core Operational Challenge
1. **Excessive Customer Abandonment**: Nearly 1 in 5 callers (**18.92%**, 946 calls) disconnect in the queue before ever connecting with an agent.
2. **Speed of Answer Volatility**: Callers wait an average of **67.52 seconds** (Average Speed of Answer, ASA), with severe wait spikes occurring during peak midday hours.
3. **Agent Performance Disparity**: Noticeable variances exist across the 8-agent roster in terms of resolution rates (88.89% to 90.64%), speed of answer (65.33s to 70.99s), and average customer satisfaction (3.33 to 3.47 out of 5.00).
4. **Lack of Performance Quadrant Visibility**: Management currently lacks a quadrant mapping **Average Handle Time (Talk Duration)** against **Calls Answered**, making it difficult to distinguish fast call-churning agents from thorough, high-resolution specialists.

## Key Stakeholders
- **Claire (Call Centre Manager)**: Primary project champion; requires an executive overview of daily operational health, peak hourly arrival curves, and agent scorecards for quarterly coaching.
- **Team Leads & Shift Supervisors**: Require drill-down capability into individual agent metrics (Answer Rate, Speed of Answer, CSAT by Topic).
- **Chief Customer Officer (CCO)**: Focused on customer retention, Net Promoter Score (NPS), and preventing customer churn sparked by poor support interactions.

## Key Questions to Answer for Claire
1. What is the overall customer satisfaction (CSAT) score, and how does it correlate with hold times and issue resolution?
2. What are the macro ratios for calls answered versus abandoned, and when do abandonment peaks occur?
3. How does inbound demand fluctuate by time of day, day of week, and inquiry topic?
4. What is the average speed of answer (ASA), and where are the primary queue bottlenecks?
5. How do agents position within the **Performance Quadrant** (Average Handle Time vs Calls Answered)?
6. Which actionable staffing and technology initiatives will yield the highest reduction in call abandonment?
