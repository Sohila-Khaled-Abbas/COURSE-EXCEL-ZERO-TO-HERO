# 1. Business Context & Problem Statement

## The Business Context
OmniConnect Telecom operates a customer support contact center handling technical support, billing inquiries, contract administration, general admin, and streaming issues. Customer satisfaction scores have slipped, and executive leadership has commissioned an operational review to assess customer support efficiency and agent accountability.

## The Core Problem
1. **High Abandonment**: Nearly 1 in 5 callers (18.92%, 946 calls) disconnect before speaking to an agent.
2. **Speed of Answer Volatility**: Callers wait an average of 67.52 seconds, with significant spikes during peak hours.
3. **Agent Performance Disparity**: Meaningful variances in resolution rates and customer satisfaction exist across the 8-agent roster.

## Key Stakeholders
- **Head of Customer Operations**: Focused on SLA compliance, average handle time, and staffing allocation.
- **Call Center Team Leads**: Requiring individual agent scorecards for performance coaching.
- **Chief Customer Officer (CCO)**: Focused on Net Promoter Score (NPS) and Customer Satisfaction (CSAT).

## Key Questions to Answer
1. What is the overall call answer and resolution efficiency across Q1 2021?
2. Which customer topics drive the greatest call volume and lowest satisfaction?
3. What is the hourly call arrival pattern, and does staffing match volume peaks?
4. Are certain agents outperforming peers in resolution rate or speed of answer?
