# 9. Project Retrospective & Digital Accelerator Competencies

## Core Lessons Learned as a PwC Digital Accelerator
1. **The Role of the Digital Accelerator**: In modern professional services, data analysts are not passive report builders—they are catalysts who leverage digital tools (Power BI, Power Pivot, DAX, Power Query) to transform unstructured operational chaos into actionable business strategy.
2. **Upskilling in the Digital Age**: Moving beyond basic spreadsheets to scalable semantic models empowers leadership (like Claire) to simulate capacity shifts, monitor SLAs in real time, and protect customer retention.
3. **Data Empathy & Forensic Quality**: Grounding technical analysis in operational reality (understanding why 946 nulls exist) prevents erroneous data cleaning and builds trust with executive stakeholders.

## Technical Competencies Demonstrated
1. **Forensic Data Quality Auditing**: Diagnosed that 946 null values in `Speed of answer in seconds`, `AvgTalkDuration`, and `Satisfaction rating` were intentional operational markers of abandoned calls rather than corrupt data loss.
2. **Advanced Data Modeling & DAX Semantic Layer**: Implemented explicit DAX measures (`DIVIDE`, `CALCULATE`, `DISTINCTCOUNT`, `FILTER`) to ensure robust calculation logic without divide-by-zero errors.
3. **Operational Visual Analytics & UI Architecture**: Structured a 2-page dashboard layout (**The KPI Dashboard** and **The Detail Page**) featuring the **Agent Performance Quadrant** (Talk Duration vs Volume).
4. **Interactive Dashboard Engineering**: Synchronized multiple Pivot Tables through Slicer Report Connections and automated state resets with a VBA macro.
5. **Strategic Management Consulting Delivery**: Formulated targeted workforce management, self-service deflection, and callback queuing recommendations projected to reduce queue abandonment by 35–50%.
