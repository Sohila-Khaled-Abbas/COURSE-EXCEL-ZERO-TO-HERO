# Lesson 7.4: Enterprise Business Systems Architecture (ERP, CRM, HRIS)

> [!abstract] Learning Objective
> Understand where corporate data originates by exploring ERP, CRM, and HRIS systems, and trace the flow of transactional data to analytical reporting.

> 🎥 **Video Chapter**: [Chapter 7 – Importing Data & Data Cleaning (3:54:03)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uv1bxe2gdnU&t=14043s)

## Enterprise Systems Matrix
| System Type | Primary Systems | Typical Data Stored | Analyst Use Cases |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CRM** (Customer Relationship Management) | Salesforce, HubSpot | Leads, Accounts, Call logs, Support tickets | Sales pipeline, Customer retention, Call center analytics |
| **ERP** (Enterprise Resource Planning) | SAP, Oracle NetSuite | Invoices, Inventory, General Ledger, Procurement | Revenue reconciliation, Supply chain, Cost analysis |
| **HRIS** (Human Resources Info System) | Workday, BambooHR | Employees, Headcount, Payroll, Turnover | Attrition modeling, Diversity metrics, Performance scorecards |
