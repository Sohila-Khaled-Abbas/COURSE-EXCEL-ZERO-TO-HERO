# DATEDIF Function
> [!abstract] Purpose
> Calculates the number of days, months, or years between two dates.

## Syntax
```excel
=DATEDIF(start_date, end_date, unit)
```
- `"D"`: Full days.
- `"M"`: Complete calendar months.
- `"Y"`: Complete calendar years.
